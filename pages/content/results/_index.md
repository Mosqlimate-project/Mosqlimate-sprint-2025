## 📈 Official Report and Policy Scenarios

The insights derived from the ensemble modeling were used to generate the **"Relatório Técnico: Desafio Infodengue–Mosqlimate (IMDC)"**, a high-impact technical report for the **Brazilian Ministry of Health**. This report presents **ensemble predictions for dengue and chikungunya for the upcoming 2026–2027 season**, supporting public health preparedness.

* **Technical Report (Portuguese Only):** The official report detailing the seasonal scenarios is available on Zenodo: [https://zenodo.org/records/24008622](https://zenodo.org/records/24008622)
